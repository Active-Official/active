# Code of Conduct (C-064)

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ACTIVE project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Positive behaviors: empathy and kindness; respectful disagreement;
graceful acceptance of constructive criticism; focus on what is
best for the community; attention to the impact of shipped
configuration language on downstream users.

Unacceptable behaviors: sexualized language or imagery; trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks;
public or private harassment; publishing others' private
information without permission; any conduct reasonably considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (repository, issue
tracker, discussions, releases) and when representing the project
publicly. Violations may lead to warning, temporary interaction
limits, or a permanent ban, decided by the repository owner (see
`.github/CODEOWNERS`).

Report through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/RatioArtificiosa/solid-lang/security/advisories/new)
with a `[CoC]` title prefix — never by public issue. Advisory
reports are visible only to people with repository security access
(the owner, plus anyone explicitly granted it); they are
acknowledged within 3 days and handled confidentially. If you
cannot use GitHub, ask someone who can to file on your behalf.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version
2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
with ACTIVE-specific reporting and scope.
